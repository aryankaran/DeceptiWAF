OUR APPROACH

Don't just block attackers —
trap them.

DeceptiWAF combines a scoring WAF with a deception honeypot. Instead of rejecting brute-forcers with a 401, we lie "Login Successful" and serve a fake dashboard — then watch every move they make on the SOC feed.

Here's the philosophy that shapes everything else in this deck. A traditional WAF blocks an attacker and moves on — the attacker simply retries from a new IP, a new payload, a new tool. We don't want that loop. So DeceptiWAF runs a scoring WAF in parallel with a deception honeypot. When a brute-forcer fails three times, the fourth attempt is told "Login Successful" and they're served a fully-rendered fake dashboard with a deterministic fake student profile. They think they're in. Meanwhile, every click, every form submission, every API call they make is streamed live to the SOC dashboard with their IP, geolocation, and fingerprint. Block, trap, and watch — three defenses working together instead of one alone.
