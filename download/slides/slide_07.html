The SOC dashboard is where everything comes together for the defender. Through Socket.io, every WAF block, every brute-force attempt, every honeypot trap surfaces in real time — no refresh needed. Each block event shows the attacker’s IP, the attack family, the score, the matched rules with their point values, and the offending payload. Honeypot traps are highlighted in purple and include the fake identity we served — so admins can verify the deception is working as intended. We resolve every attacker IP through ip-api.com to get city, country, ISP, and a flag emoji, which makes attribution tangible: “this attack came from a datacenter in Ashburn, Virginia” is far more actionable than “10.0.0.7 hit us”. The top-attackers catalog and trapped-attackers panel let admins reset honeypot state on demand. Three admin-only REST APIs expose the same data programmatically.
