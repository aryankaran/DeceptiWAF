DeceptiWAF

A Deception-Based Web Application Firewall with Brute-Force Honeypot

Node.js Express Socket.io

~Aryan Karan · B.Tech. Project.

Good afternoon everyone. Today I'm presenting DeceptiWAF — a deception-based Web Application Firewall built as our Software Engineering project at Kalyani University. The core idea is simple but powerful: instead of just blocking attackers, we trap them. DeceptiWAF pairs a scoring WAF with a brute-force honeypot that lies "Login Successful" to attackers and serves them a fake dashboard — then watches every move they make on the SOC feed. Over the next ten slides we'll walk through the threat landscape, our deception philosophy, the four-module architecture, the scoring engine, the honeypot flow, the live SOC dashboard, anti-evasion techniques, and our test results. Let's get started.
